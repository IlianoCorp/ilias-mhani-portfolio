Sustainability & awareness

Climate Fresk Workshop

ENSEEIHT

Participated in a Climate Fresk workshop at ENSEEIHT, a collaborative exercise designed to understand the mechanisms behind climate change by connecting its main causes and consequences.

What I took from it

The workshop helped me better understand the systemic nature of climate change and the links between technology, human activity and environmental impact.

Circular Economy Conference

Attended a conference on circular economy exploring how companies and industries can rethink the use of materials, resources and production flows to reduce waste and improve long-term sustainability.

What I took from it

It introduced me to the idea that engineering decisions are not only about technical performance, but also about how resources are used throughout the life cycle of a product or system.
